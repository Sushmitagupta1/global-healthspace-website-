Privacy Policy.

How Global Healthspace Studio Private Limited collects, uses and protects information shared through this website.

Last updated: 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Global Healthspace Studio Private Limited (“we”, “us”, “our”) handles information that you provide through this website. This is a general informational website for an architecture and healthcare planning practice.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the information you choose to send us through the enquiry form or by email, which may include:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your telephone number
  • Your organisation or company name
  • Project-related information that you provide
  • Any other details you include in your message

3. How We Use Information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Respond to your enquiry
  • Understand your project requirements
  • Communicate with you about potential or ongoing work
  • Keep records of enquiries for our internal purposes

4. Form Submissions

The enquiry form on this website is currently a demonstration form. It does not automatically transmit your data to a server. When a form submission is made in this version of the site, no information is stored or sent. Before any form is connected to a live submission service, this policy will be updated.

5. Cookies and Analytics

This website is built as a static website. It does not set tracking cookies for advertising. If analytics or additional tools are added in future, this policy will be updated to describe them.

6. Sharing of Information

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We may share information where required by law, or with professional advisers and service providers strictly for the purpose of responding to your enquiry.

7. Data Retention and Security

We retain enquiry information only for as long as needed for the purposes described above. We take reasonable steps to protect the information we hold, though no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

8. Your Rights

You may contact us at any time to ask about the information you have shared or to request that it be corrected or deleted, subject to applicable law.

9. Third-Party Links

This website may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those websites.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The current version will always be available on this page.
