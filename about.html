About Us

Designing spaces that care.

Global Healthspace Studio is a healthcare-focused design practice based in Ahmedabad, Gujarat. We bring together architectural thinking, healthcare planning and technical expertise to create environments that support patients, caregivers, medical professionals and the communities they serve.

Our Perspective

Healthcare architecture is more than designing a building.

It is about understanding how people move, heal, work, wait, recover and interact within a complex environment. Our work focuses on creating thoughtful healthcare spaces where planning, functionality, technology, efficiency and human experience come together.

The studio brings together architecture, healthcare planning and engineering expertise into a single, focused practice dedicated to healthcare environments.

Our Vision

To shape healthier environments through better design.

We envision healthcare spaces that are human-centred, functionally efficient, technically integrated and adaptable to changing healthcare needs.

Our Mission

Integrating architecture, healthcare planning and technical expertise.

Our approach is built around the relationship between people, processes and place, balancing operational requirements with comfort, clarity and dignity.

Sustainability

Designing responsibly.

Healthcare buildings operate continuously and have significant environmental and operational requirements. Our design approach can consider natural daylight, climate-responsive planning, energy efficiency, water management, material selection, landscape integration, indoor environmental quality and long-term adaptability.

Certifications and performance claims are added only where a project has actually achieved them.
